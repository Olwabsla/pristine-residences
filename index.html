Who we are

Most stays make you choose. This one doesn't.

A hotel is easy, but it gives you one room. A rented house gives you space, and with it the power bills, the cleaning and the cooking.

Pristine Residences is built so you get both. Families on long visits, teams on assignment, friends travelling together: anyone who needs room to live and work properly, for a few nights or a few months.

What Pristine Residences takes care of
Security
A gated compound with manned security, 24 hours a day.
Power
Constant electricity, backed by two generators (400 kVA and 200 kVA) and a personal inverter for your villa alone.
Connection
Wi-Fi around the clock, and the full DSTV bouquet.
Space
Four bedrooms, two living rooms and an office over three floors, plus a shared gym and pool.
Chores
Housekeeping included. A cook or caterer whenever you want one.
